# RNG Shooter Roster

16 Classes: 5 Common · 5 Rare · 3 Epic · 3 Legendary. Rarer Classes get more options, never higher stats ([[0001-rarity-gives-options-not-stats|ADR 0001]]). Everyday jobs at the bottom, getting weirder toward the top; the props are the weapons (inspired by [[Randomizer]]). Glossary: [[CONTEXT]].

**Starter Class:** Janitor.

## Common: each ability does one thing
| Class | Weapon | Q | E | F (Ultimate) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Janitor** | Pressure Washer (short-mid stream, light knockback) | *Wet Floor*: place a sign; anyone running over it slips into a slide | *Mop Swing*: melee shove | *Flood*: a wave rolls down the street, pushing players and breaking weak walls |
| **Line Cook** | Hot Sauce Shotgun (close, short burn) | *Pan Toss*: thrown pan, brief stun on hit | *Snack Break*: eat a burger to heal | *Grease Fire*: big burning zone |
| **Mail Carrier** | Parcel Launcher (bouncing box grenades) | *Return to Sender*: a sticky parcel that explodes after a delay | *Express*: speed boost | *Bulk Delivery*: a mail truck drives through the Map, smashing buildings |
| **Crossing Guard** | Hand Cannon (slow, heavy pistol) | *Stop Sign*: place a temporary wall | *Whistle*: nearby enemies are slowed briefly | *Rush Hour*: a line of cars streams across a marked lane |
| **Birdwatcher** | Long Rifle (scoped, binocular-themed) | *Bird Call*: a decoy that makes fake footsteps and shots | *Bush Suit*: blend in while standing still | *The Flock*: birds swarm an area, revealing and pecking enemies |

## Rare: one ability with a second use
| Class | Weapon | Q | E | F (Ultimate) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pizza Courier** | Disc Launcher (pizza discs that ricochet) | *Hot Slice*: eat it to heal **or** throw it to burn | *Scooter Dash*: a short dash | *Extra Large*: a giant rolling pizza flattens its path |
| **Plumber** | Plunger Crossbow (bolts stick to targets) | *Plunger Line*: pull yourself to a wall **or** pull an enemy to you | *Pipe Bomb*: a timed explosive | *Sewer Run*: dive into the ground and pop up anywhere nearby |
| **Gym Bro** | Protein LMG (big mag, slow to aim) | *Bench Press*: hold a weight plate as a shield, **release to throw it** | *Pre-Workout*: speed boost | *Max Out*: bulk up; punches break walls and knock players back |
| **Weather Presenter** | Umbrella Shotgun | *Umbrella*: fire normally, **or open it as a shield** | *Gust*: push enemies away **or** launch yourself | *Storm Front*: Map-wide rain that slows everyone and shows footsteps |
| **Stage Magician** | Card Thrower (fast, light projectiles) | *Swap*: trade places with your top hat decoy | *Top Hat*: place a decoy **or** recall it | *Grand Finale*: a ring of exploding doves around you |

## Epic: Passive + one ability with two modes
| Class | Weapon | Passive | Q | E | F (Ultimate) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Rogue Mascot** | T-Shirt Cannon (arcing splash) | *Bouncy Suit*: no fall damage, a small double jump | *Balloon*: float up, **pop it to drop and slam** | *Hug*: grab an enemy and hold them for a moment | *Parade*: ride a float that fires T-shirts all around |
| **Retired Wizard** | Spell Book (fireballs, burn) | *Old Habits*: kills drop a small mana orb that refunds cooldown | *Blink*: teleport forward **or** back to where you blinked from | *Frost Ring*: a slowing nova | *Meteor*: call down a meteor that craters the Map |
| **Beekeeper** | Hive Gun (bees, damage over time) | *Marked*: enemies you sting are outlined for you | *Smoker*: smoke cloud that **heals you or blinds enemies** | *Honey Trap*: a sticky puddle | *The Swarm*: a bee cloud that chases the nearest enemy |

## Legendary: Passive + several multi-use abilities + an Ultimate that changes how you play
| Class | Weapon | Passive | Q | E | F (Ultimate) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Haunted Vending Machine** | Can Launcher (soda grenades) | *Out of Order*: stand still and you look like a prop | *Dispense*: drop a soda that **heals whoever drinks it, or explodes when shaken** | *Coin Return*: suck coins (and nearby enemies) toward you | *Jackpot*: rolls a random Ultimate from another Class (RNG inside RNG) |
| **Alien Tourist** | Ray Camera (beam, mid range) | *Souvenir*: photographing (hitting) an enemy shows their health | *Flash Photo*: a flashbang **or** a snapshot that marks everyone in frame | *Hover*: float briefly **or** slow-fall | *Abduction*: a UFO mode for a few seconds; beam players up and drop them |
| **Time-Lost Knight** | Crossbow + Kite Shield | *Plate Armour*: the front blocks the first bullet each few seconds | *Shield*: block, **bash** or **throw and recall** | *Charge*: sprint into enemies; misses leave you stunned | *Dragon Form*: become a dragon for a few seconds and breathe fire from above |

## Notes
- **Combat ranges:** close (Line Cook, Weather Presenter, Gym Bro up close), mid (Janitor, Mail Carrier, Plumber, Pizza Courier, Magician, Mascot, Beekeeper, Wizard, Alien), long (Birdwatcher, Crossing Guard, Knight crossbow).
- **Map destruction:** Flood, Bulk Delivery, Extra Large, Max Out, Meteor and Dragon Form break buildings.
- **Removed from the old 4:** Rookie, Bruiser, Twin Fang, Phantom. Some of their abilities (grenade, flashbang, cloak, airstrike) could be reused under new names.
