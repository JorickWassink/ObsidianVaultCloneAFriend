# Clone a Friend: Bear Boss redesign, Gemini handout

A ready-to-paste brief for Gemini to draw the new Boss 1 (Brown Bear). It runs in two stages: **Stage 1** gives 3 concept options, and **Stage 2** gives a turnaround sheet of the option you pick. Earlier history is in [[Clone a Friend Boss Restyle]] and [[Clone a Friend Boss 1 Redesign]].

## How to use
1. Download the reference images below and attach them to the Gemini chat.
2. Paste the **Stage 1 prompt**.
3. Pick an option (or ask for tweaks), then paste the **Stage 2 prompt** with the option number filled in.
4. Bring the chosen turnaround back here for the 3D build.

## Stage 1 prompt (3 options)

```
You are a character concept artist for a Roblox game. Design a NEW boss character: a grumpy brown bear. This is the first boss of the game, a cartoony, colourful Roblox game where players fight bosses with their pets.

GOAL
Draw 3 different 2D concept options of the bear boss, each shown full-body in one front 3/4 view, side by side on one image, on a plain light grey background, labelled 1, 2 and 3.

WHAT THE OLD DESIGN GOT WRONG (fix all of these)
- It didn't feel like a boss: it looked like a regular mascot, not a threat.
- Its silhouette was boring: a stack of boxes that doesn't read from far away.
- Its details looked rough: jagged fur clumps, flat square belly patch, props that looked stuck on.

MUST HAVE
- Species: brown bear, instantly recognisable as a bear.
- Theme: HONEY. Use honey as the character's signature (for example a honey jar or honeycomb as a weapon/armour/trophy, honey drips on paws or fur, a honeycomb pattern, bee details). Make honey part of the silhouette, not a tiny accessory.
- Attitude: GRUMPY. The resting face is a grumpy scowl.
- Face: cartoon face that reads from far away. Big readable eyes with a heavy angled brow, a mouth showing one or two small fangs. NO rows of realistic teeth, NO glowing eyes.
- No glowing parts at all. Plain, bold, saturated colours with clear colour blocking.
- A strong, unique silhouette: you should recognise it as a black shape alone.
- Boss scale: about 20 studs tall, roughly 4 times a Roblox player's height. It must look big and heavy next to a player.
- Buildable in 3D for Roblox: shapes made of simple, chunky, low-poly forms (big rounded blocks, wedges, cylinders), with no tiny detail, thin strands of fur or realistic textures.
- Readable limbs: clear arms, legs, head and torso so it can be rigged and animated (walk, paw strike, jumping ground slam).

THE 3 OPTIONS (each mixes "cartoon villain" and "imposing endgame monster" differently; see the attached reference images for the monster feel)
1. CARTOON VILLAIN: head about 1/4 to 1/3 of total height, soft pear-shaped belly (no muscles), bendy arms with big paws and big feet. Funny but clearly the bad guy.
2. IMPOSING MONSTER: like the attached references: chunky, top-heavy, massive shoulders and forearms, small head sunk into the shoulders, short thick legs, heavy chunky armour-like plates (made of honeycomb, wax or wood). Still the cartoon face described above.
3. IN BETWEEN: cartoon proportions and personality with some of the monster's mass and chunky plating.

Under each option, write one line: its name, the main honey idea, and the colour palette (3 to 5 colours).
Do not copy any existing character; the references are for scale and chunkiness only.
```

## Stage 2 prompt (turnaround of the chosen option)

```
Great. Take OPTION [N] exactly as drawn (same proportions, colours, props and face) and make a character turnaround sheet for a 3D modeller.

- One image, plain light grey background, the same character shown 4 times side by side at the same scale and height: FRONT, FRONT 3/4, SIDE (profile), BACK.
- Neutral standing pose (A-pose or relaxed arms slightly away from the body) so the limbs are clearly visible from every angle.
- Grumpy resting face in every view.
- Keep every detail consistent between views: same number of plates, same honey props, same colours, same placement. The back view must clearly show what's on the back.
- Draw a simple Roblox player silhouette next to the front view for scale (bear is about 4 times taller).
- Flat, clean colours; no glow, no busy textures, no background scenery.
- Below the sheet, list the main shapes and colours (with hex codes) so it can be rebuilt in 3D.
```

If the views drift between images (different props or colours), reply: "Keep option [N] identical across all 4 views; only the angle changes."

## Decisions behind this handout (grilling, 2026-11-16)
| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Start point | Fresh redesign, not a tweak of the restyled original |
| Process | 3 options first, then a turnaround of the chosen one |
| Views | Front, front 3/4, side, back (no expression sheet or action pose for now) |
| Image style | 2D concept art |
| Skeleton | Free body shape; animations get remade afterwards |
| Keep | Honey theme and grumpy attitude; picnic bandana/cape dropped |
| Body | Cartoon villain, varied across the 3 options against Steal An Egg's top-heavy look |
| Face | Cartoon eyes + angled brow + 1-2 small fangs |
| Glow | None |
| Problems to fix | Doesn't look like big models in Steal An Egg, not boss-like, boring silhouette, ugly details |
| References | Steal An Egg endgame pets (wiki images) |
