# RNG Shooter

A fast free-for-all PvP shooter where the Class you play is decided by a Roll. Rarer Classes give more options in a fight, never a guaranteed win.

## Language

### Rolling

**Class**:
A playable loadout of one weapon plus a Q ability, an E ability and an F Ultimate (and a Passive from Epic up), belonging to exactly one Rarity.
_Avoid_: Character, style, hero, kit

**Weapon**:
A gun type a Class carries; several Classes may share one, chosen for what fits each Class.
_Avoid_: Gun, primary

**Ultimate**:
A Class's F ability, charged by kills, damage dealt and time in the Match.
_Avoid_: Ult, super, special

**Passive**:
An always-on effect that Epic and Legendary Classes have on top of their Q, E and F.
_Avoid_: Perk, trait, buff

**Rarity**:
One of the four tiers a Class belongs to: Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary. A higher Rarity means more options in a fight, not higher raw stats.
_Avoid_: Tier, grade, level

**Roll**:
One random draw that picks a Rarity by weight, then a Class within it, replacing the Equipped Class. Bought with Cash.
_Avoid_: Spin, pull, gacha

**Cash**:
The single currency, earned by actions in a Match and by daily quests, spent on Rolls.
_Avoid_: Coins, money, credits

**Daily Quest**:
One of three tasks a day that pay Cash; finishing all three grants a Lucky Roll.
_Avoid_: Mission, challenge, task

**Lucky Roll**:
A Roll that cannot land on Common.
_Avoid_: Premium roll, super spin

**Pity**:
The guarantee that a player who has rolled long enough without a Legendary gets one.
_Avoid_: Bad-luck protection, mercy

**Level**:
A player's account progression from playing Matches; it unlocks extra Locker slots.
_Avoid_: Rank, XP level, tier

**Equipped Class**:
The Class a player takes into the next Match.
_Avoid_: Current class, active class

**Starter Class**:
The fixed Common Class every new player begins with.
_Avoid_: Default class, free class

**Vaulted**:
A Class temporarily removed from the Roll pool; players who already have it keep it.
_Avoid_: Disabled, banned, retired

**Locker**:
The player's limited set of slots where Classes are saved to equip later.
_Avoid_: Inventory, storage, saved classes

### Match

**Match**:
One timed free-for-all round between players only, scored by player kills.
_Avoid_: Game, round, session

**Finish Bonus**:
Cash paid for staying until a Match ends; leaving early forfeits it.
_Avoid_: Completion reward, end bonus

**Map**:
One hand-built, destructible arena a Match is played on; chosen by vote during the Intermission.
_Avoid_: Level, arena, stage

**Intermission**:
The short gap between Matches when everyone is back in the menu, can Roll, and votes on the next Map.
_Avoid_: Lobby, break, downtime

**Practice Range**:
A space outside the Match where a player tries their Equipped and Locker Classes on respawning test dummies; kills there count for nothing.
_Avoid_: Training, dummy area

## Rejected concepts

**Modifier**: Rolled variants or auras stacked on top of a Class (Sol's RNG style). Deliberately not part of this game; see ADR 0002.
