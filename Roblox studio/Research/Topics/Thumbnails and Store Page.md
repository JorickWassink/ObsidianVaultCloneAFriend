## Principles
- **Sell the hook in 2–3 words** (EARNS OFFLINE) rather than describing the game.
- **A big readable subject** on a blurred or simple background.
- **The Roblox avatar holding or looking at the prize.** The prize is the collectible.
- **Thick outlines on all text** so it reads at small sizes.
- **Show platform support** if you're mobile/console friendly.

## Game description example
[[Fisch]] describes itself as an "ultimate fishing adventure", stresses exploration and progression, and gives a huge variety number (400,000+ variations). **Big variety numbers make a strong pitch.**
