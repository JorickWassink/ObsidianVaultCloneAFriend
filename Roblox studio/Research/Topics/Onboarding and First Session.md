# Onboarding and First Session

## What the games do
| Game | First-session design |
|---|---|
| [[Fisch]] | Tutorial NPC (Pierre) on the beach. The first goal is clear and close: **400 C$ for a Rowboat** opens the world. A GPS item costs 100 C$. |
| [[Catch Bugs!]] | A tutorial gives basic gear. The **Finder questline** asks for "Catch 1 Honey Bee, Catch 1 Mosquito…", teaching the index one bug at a time. |
| [[Rivals]] | **Beginner tasks**, each worth 1–3 keys: visit the shooting range, break 5 targets, slide, equip a scythe, dash, throw a grenade, play a duel, eliminate 5, win a duel. Every mechanic is taught by doing it. Then a **Beginner 2v2** queue only for new players (with wall-hack at round start). |
| [[Storage Hunters Open World]] | The starter area (Junk Yard, $0 net worth), and auctions are tuned to "rarely lose money". |
| [[Steal An Egg]] | The first biome (Forest) is right next to your base with the weakest guardian (Chicken). |
| [[Grow a Garden]] | Four seeds are always in stock (Carrot 10¢, Strawberry, Blueberry, Tomato), so you can always do the loop. |
| [[Steal a Brainrot]] | Commons spawn ~55% of the time and cost as little as $25, so your base fills fast. First rebirth was made cheaper after launch. |

### Second batch
| Game | First-session design |
|---|---|
| [[Adopt Me!]] | A **free Starter Egg** (Cat or Dog) for new players, and a simple role choice: baby or parent. |
| [[Blox Fruits]] | Pick a team (Pirate or Marine), each with its own starter island. **Signage explains dash and sprint keys**, and the first enemies are very weak. |
| [[99 Nights in the Forest]] | An in-game tutorial at the start of a run, and a **guaranteed coal/log pile** almost ensures reaching fire level 2. |
| [[Murder Mystery 2]] | No tutorial needed: the rules are understood from the role screen. Innocents always have a job (collect coins). |
| [[Pet Simulator 99]] | Area 1 has the shop, mailbox, rewards and index together. Area 2 costs only 900 coins, a few minutes of play. |
| [[Blue Lock Rivals]] | **A free starter character** (Isagi, the anime's protagonist, plus his flow) that's simple and good for learning. Floor arrows in the lobby lead to the Play terminal. Ranked unlocks at Level 20. |

## Principles
1. **Teach by doing**, with tiny rewards per action ([[Rivals]] beginner tasks).
2. **The first goal is visible and reachable in minutes** (the [[Fisch]] boat).
3. **Never let the loop stall:** guaranteed stock ([[Grow a Garden]]), profitable early auctions ([[Storage Hunters Open World]]).
4. **Protect new players from veterans** ([[Rivals]] beginner queue).
5. **Introduce the collection log early** ([[Catch Bugs!]] Finder quests).
6. **Make the first prestige fast** ([[Steal a Brainrot]] lowered rebirth 1 and 2).

## First-session checklist for my game
- [ ] Under 30s to the first action of the core verb
- [ ] First sale or reward in under 1 min
- [ ] A visible first goal reachable in about 5–10 min
- [ ] A tutorial quest chain that teaches every system by doing it
- [ ] The starter area can't be failed
- [ ] The collection log is shown in the first session
