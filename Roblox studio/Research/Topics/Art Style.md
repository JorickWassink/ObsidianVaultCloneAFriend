**More notes:**
- **Marketing art can be far more dramatic than gameplay** ([[Blue Lock Rivals]]: illustrated banners full of VFX vs a clean pitch in play). Keep play readable, and put the drama in banners and ability effects.
- **Glow as a variant** ([[Adopt Me!]] Neon = glowing parts; Mega Neon = color-cycling glow) is the cheapest iconic variant system in the vault.
- **Horror on Roblox works best with simple shapes and darkness.** [[99 Nights in the Forest]]'s Deer is a few parts with a scary silhouette.
- **Older games revamp starter areas** ([[Blox Fruits]] First Sea, Update 30). The first impression gets re-polished over time.

## Shared traits
- **Saturated colors and high contrast.** Everything reads well on small phone screens.
- **The Roblox avatar stays front and center.** Blocky "noob" avatars appear in thumbnails ([[Fisch]], [[Catch Bugs!]]).
- **Low-poly, chunky shapes** with smooth or studded materials.
- **Glow and sparkle for value.** Gold, rainbow and emissive accents mean rare.
- **The environment stays simpler than the collectibles** in collection games ([[Steal a Brainrot]], [[Grow a Garden]]).

## The spectrum
```
Classic Roblox ─────────── Stylized ─────────── Atmospheric/realistic
Grow a Garden   Steal a Brainrot   Catch Bugs!   Steal An Egg   Storage Hunters   Fisch
(studs, baseplate)                 (bright nature) (toy + glow)  (city realism)    (fog, light)
Rivals sits apart: functional grey-box for competitive clarity.
```

**Notable:** the two biggest hits here ([[Grow a Garden]], [[Steal a Brainrot]]) use the *simplest* environments. Simple art isn't what holds a game back; the hook matters more. [[Fisch]] shows that atmosphere can make a simple loop feel premium.

## Escalation through areas
- [[Steal An Egg]] biomes: forest → lake → desert → jungle → snow → volcano → abyss ocean → prehistoric → cosmic → cherry blossom → titan temple.
- [[Catch Bugs!]] zones: grasslands → forest → night hollow → caves → desert → tomb → jewel jungle → Japanese garden → swamp → prehistoric → volcanic.
- [[Fisch]]: cozy docks → tropical → deep sea → volcanic, and more.

**Pattern:** the opening area is familiar and calm, the middle is exotic, and the end is otherworldly (space, hell, heaven). Color temperature and saturation rise as you progress.

## Mutation / effect visual language (shared across games)
| Effect | Visual |
|---|---|
| Gold | Metallic yellow plus coin/sparkle particles |
| Silver/Diamond | Reflective white/cyan |
| Rainbow | Animated cycling hue |
| Frozen/Chilled | Pale blue plus frost |
| Fiery/Infernal/Lava | Orange-red glow plus flame particles |
| Electrified | Blue-violet sparks |
| Radioactive | Lime green glow |
| Galaxy/Cosmic | Dark purple with stars |
| Evil/Bloodrot | Deep red/black |

Players already know this language from other games, so follow it.

## Choosing a direction for my game
- **Low art budget, fast content:** classic Roblox / meme-first. Put the effort into the characters.
- **Want to stand out:** atmosphere (lighting, fog, post-processing) like [[Fisch]].
- **Competitive:** grey-box clarity like [[Rivals]].
