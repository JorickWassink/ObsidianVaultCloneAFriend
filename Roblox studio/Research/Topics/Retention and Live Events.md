# Retention and Live Events

## Timers players learn
| Rhythm | Example |
|---|---|
| **Every few minutes** | [[Grow a Garden]] seed restock (5 min), [[Steal An Egg]] nest reset (5 min) |
| **Every ~30–45 min** | [[Grow a Garden]] egg restock (30 min), [[Steal An Egg]] boss (30 min), [[Catch Bugs!]] weather (45 min, lasts 5) |
| **In-game day** | [[Fisch]] day/night cycle of 25 min, fish 75% rarer outside their time |
| **Hours** | [[Fisch]] seasons ~9.6h, [[Grow a Garden]] Ascension cooldown 4h, [[Catch Bugs!]] Vivarium upgrades up to 12h |
| **Daily** | [[Rivals]] daily tasks + streak, daily shop rotation, daily ELO shield |
| **Weekly** | [[Grow a Garden]] and [[Steal a Brainrot]] Saturday updates + Admin Abuse, "Watering Wednesday", "Taco Tuesday" |
| **Seasonal** | Holiday events everywhere (Halloween, Christmas, Easter, summer) with their own currency and limited items |

**Lesson:** layer short, medium and long timers so there's always "something about to happen".

## Developer live events ("Admin Abuse")
Seen in [[Grow a Garden]] and [[Steal a Brainrot]]:
- The developer goes live right after the weekly update, spawns rare weather, restocks shops, gives luck boosts (e.g. 15×) and hands out exclusive items.
- **Early-bird rewards:** players online about an hour before get a gift (e.g. a Secret Lucky Block).
- **Collabs:** celebrity or YouTuber guests (Grow a Garden had KreekCraft, Travis Kelce and Glass Animals).
- Admin-exclusive items and cosmetics only drop at these events, which creates FOMO.

## Daily habit systems
- [[Rivals]]: daily tasks with a **streak that resets if you skip** (20 R$ to refresh tasks).
- [[Rivals]]: daily login bonuses, plus Skin Tickets so free players get at least one case per season.
- [[Grow a Garden]]: Daily Deals shop (24h).

## Long-term goals
- Index completion ([[Steal a Brainrot]]), bestiary ([[Fisch]]), discovery count ([[Catch Bugs!]]).
- Mastery contracts up to 72h per weapon ([[Rivals]]).
- Rebirth/Ascension ladders.
- Titles ([[Catch Bugs!]], [[Storage Hunters Open World]], [[Fisch]]).
- Ranked seasons ([[Rivals]]).

## Content cadence
- **Weekly updates** in [[Grow a Garden]] and [[Steal a Brainrot]] (update logs go past Update 60).
- **Rotating machines and shops** ([[Steal a Brainrot]] Craft Machine replaced by Witch Fuse, then Haunted Fuse), so limited content keeps cycling.
- **Limited-time locations** ([[Fisch]]: 16 so far).

## Second batch
- **Daily login with a flexible window** ([[Adopt Me!]]): claim every 15 hours, and rewards build over 5 days (25 → 300 Bucks).
- **Daily surged minigames** ([[Pet Simulator 99]]): 3 random minigames give 3–10× rewards each day.
- **Daily sales** ([[99 Nights in the Forest]]): 4 random classes discounted every 24h.
- **Daily quests** ([[99 Nights in the Forest]]): 3 a day (easy / moderate / hard), 15 diamonds for all.
- **2× weekends** ([[Adopt Me!]]): double earnings and double the daily cap every few months.
- **Hourly world spawns** ([[Blox Fruits]]): fruits spawn every hour (45 min on weekends).
- **10-minute day/night cycle** ([[Grow a Garden 2]]): every night rolls a moon (Gold 13%, Rainbow 6%, Blood 2%…) and opens stealing.
- **"Update parties"** ([[99 Nights in the Forest]]): matches before updates where developers spawn diamonds, the run-based version of Admin Abuse.
- **Seasonal events that reprice the economy** ([[Murder Mystery 2]]): its summer 2026 event drove an **all-time peak of 1.35M players**, 12 years after launch.
- **Long-term skill goals** ([[Pet Simulator 99]] Mastery to 99 in 14 skills, capes as trophies).
- **Groups** (clans, guilds) give players social reasons to return.

### [[Blue Lock Rivals]]
- **Admin abuse twice a week** (Wednesday and Saturday), giving away 40+ lucky spins. Saturday updates come with new codes.
- **AFK World:** a separate place that pays out while idle (cash every 5 min, a prize roll every 15 min) and is the only free source of lucky spins.
- **Quest reward bar:** finishing 8 daily quests gives 2 Lucky Style + 2 Lucky Flow spins.
- **Ranked seasons** with end-of-season rewards by division.
- **Vault and rework cycles:** characters return after reworks, which brings players back to try them.

### [[Sols RNG]]
- **Random biomes rolled every second** act as surprise server events (some are very rare, like Glitched or Cyberspace), and everyone gets a chat message.
- **Global announcements** of the rarest rolls in every server create a "it could be me" feeling.
- **Daily quests** (5,000 coins), and **the Bank** pays out stored coins at daily reset.
- **Weekly rental auras** for boss raids rotate every week.
