# Popular Gameplay Mechanics

Patterns that show up across the researched games: [[Grow a Garden]], [[Catch Bugs!]], [[Steal An Egg]], [[Rivals]], [[Steal a Brainrot]], [[Storage Hunters Open World]], [[Fisch]]. Sections 11–18 add [[Pet Simulator 99]], [[Adopt Me!]], [[Blox Fruits]], [[99 Nights in the Forest]], [[Murder Mystery 2]] and [[Grow a Garden 2]].

> [!summary] The big picture
> Six of the seven games share one skeleton: **acquire a random-valued thing → it makes money (sold or passively) → spend money on upgrades that improve the next acquisition → unlock a new area → repeat**. They differ in *how you acquire* (grow, catch, steal, bid, fish), and that verb is what makes each game feel unique. [[Rivals]] is the exception: a skill-based PvP game with cosmetic progression.

## 1. The acquisition verb (the game's identity)
| Game | Verb | Skill element | Random element |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Grow a Garden]] | Plant & wait | Timing restocks, planning the plot | Weight, mutation, variant |
| [[Catch Bugs!]] | Swing a net | Catch slider minigame | Bug rarity, weight, mutation |
| [[Fisch]] | Cast & reel | Reel bar minigame | Fish rarity, weight, mutation, attributes |
| [[Storage Hunters Open World]] | Bid at auction | Timing bar while bidding | Garage contents, mutations |
| [[Steal An Egg]] | Run & steal | Escaping the guardian | Size and color roll at hatch |
| [[Steal a Brainrot]] | Buy off a conveyor / steal | Racing other players, base defense | What spawns, mutation, traits |

**Lesson:** pick one *physical, fun verb* and wrap the RNG around it. A minigame (catch, reel, bid) makes the verb skill-based and makes it feel earned.

## 2. Value = base × multipliers
Every collection game multiplies a base value by stacking modifiers:
- **Size/weight roll:** [[Grow a Garden]] (weight², with a rare ×3–4 lucky roll), [[Steal An Egg]] (diminishing returns), [[Catch Bugs!]] and [[Fisch]] (weight shown in kg).
- **Mutation** (one per item, usually): weather-driven or random.
- **Variant/attribute** (stackable): Gold/Rainbow variants (GaG), traits ([[Steal a Brainrot]]), attributes like Shiny/Sparkling ([[Fisch]]).
- **Bad modifiers exist too:** Dirty 0.8× ([[Storage Hunters Open World]]), Glitched 0× ([[Fisch]]), Trash rarity ([[Fisch]]).

→ See [[Balancing]] for the actual formulas.

## 3. Passive income / idle layer
| Game | Idle system |
|---|---|
| [[Catch Bugs!]] | **Vivarium**: display your best bugs and earn money, even offline. The thumbnail literally says "EARNS OFFLINE". |
| [[Steal An Egg]] / [[Steal a Brainrot]] | Pets or brainrots in your base pay $/s. |
| [[Grow a Garden]] | Multi-harvest plants and pet passives keep producing. |
| [[Storage Hunters Open World]] | Customers buy from your shop, and staff automate offers. |
| [[Catch Bugs!]] | Traps catch bugs while you're away. |

**Lesson:** an idle layer gives a reason to log back in ("what did I earn?") and makes collected items *useful*, not just trophies.

## 4. Gates: what unlocks the next area
| Gate type | Example |
|---|---|
| **Single stat you train** | [[Steal An Egg]]: Speed 900 → 10K → 40K → … → 7B |
| **Discovery count** | [[Catch Bugs!]]: 60 / 70 bugs discovered to unlock zones |
| **Net worth** | [[Storage Hunters Open World]]: $750 → $7,500 → … |
| **Gear hard cap** | [[Catch Bugs!]]: net max weight; [[Fisch]]: rod stats |
| **Level** | [[Fisch]]: Level 200/350/750 rods; [[Rivals]]: Level 100 for Ranked |
| **Rebirth items** | [[Steal a Brainrot]]: specific brainrots + cash |
| **Vehicle** | [[Fisch]]: a boat to reach islands |

**Lesson:** one visible number that tells you what's next makes progression easy to understand.

## 5. Shared world events (appointment gaming)
- **Scheduled weather:** [[Catch Bugs!]] (every 45 min, 5 min long, 1 in 3 mutation chance), [[Grow a Garden]] (server-wide weather), [[Fisch]] (weather, day/night, seasons).
- **Timed shop restocks:** [[Grow a Garden]] (seeds 5 min, eggs 30 min, stock shared across all servers).
- **Boss on a timer:** [[Steal An Egg]] (Dr. Scramble every 30 min, 100-tier milestone track).
- **Rare-spawn announcements:** [[Steal a Brainrot]] (server / global feed).
- **Developer live events ("Admin Abuse"):** [[Grow a Garden]] and [[Steal a Brainrot]]. Weekly update plus a live event with rare spawns and luck boosts, and early-bird gifts for being online early.

→ See [[Retention and Live Events]].

## 6. Player interaction
| Type | Games |
|---|---|
| **Stealing** | [[Steal a Brainrot]] (bases + lock timer), [[Grow a Garden]] (steal fruit for Robux), [[Steal An Egg]] (from NPC guardians) |
| **Outbidding** | [[Steal a Brainrot]] (+50% to snipe off the conveyor) |
| **Trading/gifting** | [[Grow a Garden]] (trading ticket, gifting) |
| **Co-op events** | [[Steal An Egg]] (boss) |
| **Friend boost** | [[Grow a Garden]] (+10% per friend, max 40%) |
| **Competitive PvP** | [[Rivals]] (1v1–5v5, ranked) |

## 7. Collection & completion
- **Index/bestiary with silhouettes:** [[Steal a Brainrot]] (black silhouettes for unowned), [[Fisch]] (bestiary).
- **Completion rewards:** [[Steal a Brainrot]] (75% of a mutation index → +0.5× multiplier + base skin), [[Catch Bugs!]] (discoveries unlock zones).
- **Per-item mastery:** [[Rivals]] (weapon contracts: 5 min → 72 h for a unique charm).

## 8. Prestige / reset
- [[Steal a Brainrot]] **Rebirth:** lose cash and brainrots, gain a multiplier, slots, lock time and gear.
- [[Grow a Garden]] **Ascension:** spend sheckles plus a mutated fruit for Garden Coins → permanent upgrades. Cost grows each time, with a 4-hour cooldown.

## 9. Crafting / combining
- [[Catch Bugs!]]: potions from specific bugs.
- [[Steal a Brainrot]]: Craft/Fuse machines combine brainrots and rotate out with events.
- [[Storage Hunters Open World]]: certificates authenticate accessories (random bonus, re-roll for Robux).
- [[Fisch]]: relics enchant rods, and appraisal re-rolls fish.

## 10. Mini-systems that add depth cheaply
- **Favoriting** to protect items from mass-sell ([[Grow a Garden]]).
- **Loadouts** for pets ([[Grow a Garden]]) and weapons ([[Rivals]]).
- **Day/night & seasons** changing spawn tables ([[Fisch]]).
- **Beginner-only queue** ([[Rivals]]).
- **Perfect-catch bonus** for skilled play ([[Fisch]]).

## 11. More genres (second batch of games)
The second batch adds games with very different structures:

| Game | Structure | The verb |
|---|---|---|
| [[Pet Simulator 99]] | Classic pet sim: areas full of breakables | Point your pet swarm at coin piles |
| [[Adopt Me!]] | Social roleplay + pet raising | Care tasks (feed, sleep, walk) |
| [[Blox Fruits]] | Open-world action RPG | Fight NPCs for EXP, spend stat points |
| [[99 Nights in the Forest]] | Co-op survival **run** (resets) | Gather, fuel the fire, survive the night |
| [[Murder Mystery 2]] | Round-based social deduction | Hide/hunt depending on your secret role |
| [[Grow a Garden 2]] | Farming sequel | Plant & harvest, plus steal at night |

## 12. Fusion / combine ladders
- [[Adopt Me!]]: **4 Full Grown → 1 Neon, 4 Luminous Neon → 1 Mega Neon.** That's 16 pets for one Mega, a huge duplicate sink.
- [[Murder Mystery 2]]: **12 shards of a tier → 1 random item of the next tier.**
- [[Pet Simulator 99]]: Golden and Rainbow variants of the same pet.
- [[Steal a Brainrot]]: craft/fuse machines.

**Lesson:** duplicates should *always* have a use. A fixed N → 1 ladder is simple and keeps demand high.

## 13. Pets that scale with you
- [[Pet Simulator 99]] **Huge pets** don't have fixed stats; they're always stronger than your best normal pet. **Titanic** = 3× your best and ridable.

**Lesson:** a rare item that never becomes obsolete keeps its status value (and trade value) forever.

## 14. Builds & stat choices
- [[Blox Fruits]]: 3 stat points per level into Melee, Defense, Sword, Gun or Fruit, and **only 3 of 5 can be maxed**.
- [[99 Nights in the Forest]]: classes with starter kits and perks.
- [[Pet Simulator 99]]: enchant loadouts with diminishing returns per duplicate.

## 15. Time-of-day as a game mechanic
- [[Grow a Garden 2]]: **stealing only at night** (2 of every ~10 minutes), plus a random **moon** each night.
- [[99 Nights in the Forest]]: day = gather, night = survive the Deer.
- [[Fisch]]: day/night changes which fish spawn.

**Lesson:** a day/night cycle gives a natural rhythm of safe and dangerous phases.

## 16. Roles & rounds
- [[Murder Mystery 2]]: secret asymmetric roles (1 Murderer, 1 Sheriff, the rest Innocent), 3-minute rounds, and a **pity system** so you eventually get the rare role.
- [[Rivals]]: first to 5 rounds.

## 17. Long-term skill trees
- [[Pet Simulator 99]] **Mastery:** 14 skills, each to level 99, with a cape at 99 and a master cape for all.
- [[Blox Fruits]] **fruit awakening** via raids and fragments.
- [[Adopt Me!]] pet age stages.

## 18. Group systems
- [[Pet Simulator 99]] **clans** (donate diamonds, clan battles, 75 members).
- [[Grow a Garden 2]] **guilds** (50 members, guild quests and tokens).
- [[99 Nights in the Forest]] co-op teams of 1–5.

## 19. Gacha loadouts in PvP ([[Blue Lock Rivals]])
- **Your character and moveset come from a spin** ("Styles"), and a second spin pool gives a buff mode ("Flows").
- It still works as a competitive game because **mechanical skill (dribbling, timing, positioning) matters most**. The gacha changes *how* you play more than *how well*.
- **A flow bar** fills during the match and is usable from 30%, which gives a clutch moment. Support flows fill teammates' bars.
- **Awakening cutscenes** give each character a signature hype moment.
- **Vaulting:** overpowered characters are temporarily removed, reworked and returned, which keeps the meta healthy without taking things away forever.
- **AFK World:** a separate place that rewards idling (cash every 5 min, a prize every 15 min).

## 20. Pure RNG: one button ([[Sols RNG]])
- **The whole game is pressing Roll.** Everything else (gears, potions, biomes, talismans) changes your odds or speed.
- **Collectibles double as crafting materials:** common auras are consumed to craft luck gears.
- **The environment is a roll too:** biomes are rolled every second and change which auras you can get. Rolling a biome aura outside its biome is a rarer "Breakthrough".
- **Bonus roll every 10th roll** (2× luck), shown on the button.
- **Reveal cutscenes and announcements scale with rarity**, see [[Cutscenes]].
