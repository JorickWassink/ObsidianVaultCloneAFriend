# Idea Bank

Ideas for my own game, combined from the research. Each one notes where it comes from. These are suggestions, not decisions; move the good ones into `Features/`.

## Loop combinations
- **Catch + display + steal:** catch creatures (minigame like [[Catch Bugs!]]/[[Fisch]]), show them in your base for $/s ([[Catch Bugs!]] Vivarium), and others can steal from an unlocked base ([[Steal a Brainrot]]).
- **Auction + mutation reveal:** bid on mystery crates ([[Storage Hunters Open World]] timing bar). On opening, items roll size and mutation ([[Grow a Garden]] formula).
- **Grow + carry:** grow something big, then physically carry it to the sell point. Bigger means slower ([[Steal An Egg]] egg weight).

## Balancing starting points
- Size curve: `(W/BaseW)^0.62` with diminishing returns ([[Steal An Egg]]), or squared for hype ([[Grow a Garden]])
- Mutation chance: ~1 in 90 base, 1 in 3 during a matching weather event ([[Catch Bugs!]])
- Upgrade tiers: ~25× price for ~2.5× power ([[Steal An Egg]] treadmills)
- Upgrade wait times: cap at 12h ([[Catch Bugs!]])
