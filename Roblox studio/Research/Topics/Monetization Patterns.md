## Observations
1. **Paid server events are clever.** In [[Catch Bugs!]] one player pays and the whole server gets better mutation odds. The buyer feels generous and others see the purchase. ([[Grow a Garden]] Admin Abuse shows how hype these shared moments get.)
2. **Robux prices are flat while in-game prices explode.** In [[Grow a Garden]] the in-game price goes up 100,000,000× from Carrot to the top seed, but Robux only ~200×. **Robux is the shortcut for rare things, not cheap ones.**
3. **Bulk discount ladders** are standard ([[Rivals]] keys ≈ 4.9 → 4.5 R$ per key).
4. **Timers create purchase moments:** restocks, hatches, expansions.
5. **Cosmetic-only works for competitive games** ([[Rivals]]). Keep the base game fair.
6. **Premium currency also drops in play** ([[Storage Hunters Open World]] diamonds from tips and safes, [[Rivals]] keys from tasks) so free players stay engaged.
7. **Ads are a real option:** restocks, Daily Deals and free cosmetics for watching ads.
8. **Upgrades on things players love sell well** ([[Adopt Me!]] ride/fly potions on a favorite pet).
9. **Trading economies make cosmetics last for years** ([[Murder Mystery 2]], [[Adopt Me!]]). Paid items keep value because players trade them.
10. **Luck passes are the classic pet-sim purchase** ([[Pet Simulator 99]]). Make them stackable and permanent.
11. **Group features are a monetization layer:** clan/guild creation and member slots.
12. **Sell better odds rather than the item** ([[Blue Lock Rivals]]): Robux packs and lucky spins remove the bottom rarity tiers.
13. **Emotes and taunts sell in competitive games** ([[Blue Lock Rivals]] toxic and anime emotes, [[Rivals]] finishers).

## Ethics / policy notes (for my own game)
- Roblox requires **loot box odds to be disclosed** for paid random items. [[Rivals]] cases and [[Grow a Garden]] eggs show odds on the wiki/in-game.
- Avoid pay-to-win in PvP. Paid *convenience* (restocks, timers) is better received.
- See the `roblox-monetization-systems` skill for implementation (ProcessReceipt etc.).
