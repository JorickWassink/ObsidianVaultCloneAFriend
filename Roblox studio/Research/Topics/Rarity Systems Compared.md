## Colors (where the wiki states them)
[[Catch Bugs!]] uses:
- Common: white with a black outline
- Uncommon: bright green `#00FF66`
- Rare: sky blue `#00BFFF`
- Elusive: coral `#FF6B6B`
- Legendary: orange `#FFA500`
- Exotic: rainbow gradient
- Mythical: purple `#8A2BE2`
- Celestial: deep blue `#0A0096`
- Zenith: lime `#CFFF54`
- Supreme: amber `#D18B00`
- Primordial: silver with an outline

[[Grow a Garden]] shop pills: Common grey, Rare blue, Legendary yellow.

**The convention most players know:** grey/white → green → blue → purple → orange/gold → red/pink → rainbow/animated.

## Observations
- **Short ladders at the bottom, long at the top.** Games keep adding tiers *above* (Secret, OG, Eternal, Divine, Primordial) as updates release stronger content. **Leave room at the top of your ladder.**
- **"Secret" tiers** appear in 3 games: spawns are announced, and the tier usually has no fixed position in the shop.
- **Event/limited rarities sit outside the ladder** (Event, Admin, Limited, Festive), so they don't break the order.
- **One ladder for every system** ([[Steal An Egg]] uses the same names for pets, treadmills and trails), so players learn it once.
- **Trash below Common** ([[Fisch]]) is funny and makes Common feel like a win.
- **Content is often concentrated at high rarities** ([[Steal An Egg]]: 8 common/uncommon pets vs 51 Secret).

### Second batch observations
- **The older, trading-focused games use short ladders** ([[Adopt Me!]] 5, [[Murder Mystery 2]] 5). The value comes from *variants* (Neon, Mega Neon) and *scarcity*, not from more tiers.
- **Pet sims grow upward with size tiers** ([[Pet Simulator 99]] Huge → Titanic → Gargantuan) instead of adding rarity names.
- **Variants on top of rarity** are everywhere: Golden/Rainbow/Shiny ([[Pet Simulator 99]]), Neon/Mega ([[Adopt Me!]]), Big/Mega/Rainbow ([[Grow a Garden 2]]).

- **Theme the top tier names** ([[Blue Lock Rivals]] uses the sports terms "World Class" and "Master" instead of generic fantasy names). It makes the ladder feel part of the game's world.
- **No Common tier at all** ([[Blue Lock Rivals]] starts at Rare), so even the worst pull sounds decent.
