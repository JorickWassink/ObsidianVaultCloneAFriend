# Storage Hunters: Open World

![Quick Sell storefront|300](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/storagehunters/images/0/08/Quick_Sell.png/revision/latest) ![Shopping Mall|360](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/storagehunters/images/0/0a/Shopping_Mall.png/revision/latest)

## In one sentence
Like the TV show: you bid on storage garages against NPCs, haul the contents back in your vehicle, and sell the items from your own shop to customers. Your **Net Worth** unlocks bigger and better auction areas.

## Core loop
**Auction → Loot → Transport → Sell → Progress** (the wiki's own words)

- **Physical logistics:** items must be carried or driven home, and vehicles have **capacity**. That turns "inventory" into a fun driving trip.
- **Your shop is your base.** You place shelves and customers make offers, and staff can automate it.

## Popular mechanics worth stealing
| Mechanic | How it works | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Timing-bar bidding** | Press Bid while a moving marker is inside a colored target zone. The zone's color and behavior change with the garage contents and mutations. | Turns a menu action into a skill minigame, and the colored zone hints at loot quality. |
| **NPC bidders** | You compete against AI, not players. | No griefing, and it always works with few players online. |
| **Net Worth gates** | Areas need Net Worth (Junk Yard $0 → Back Alley $750 → Power Plant $7,500 → …), and garages inside also have tiers. | Net worth counts what you own, so collecting itself is progress. |
| **Open world with themed areas** | 20+ areas: Junk Yard, Back Alley, Power Plant (ferry island), Farmyard, Shipyard, Lucky Beach, Business Bay, Mines, Cargo Ship, Jurassic, Alien Invasion, Wild West, Shopping Mall, Lake (fishing), Museum, Bank, Police Station… | Exploration and variety, and each garage type has its own loot theme. |
| **Customer offers + Staff** | Customers make offers on displayed items, and a Shop Assistant can negotiate. | A tycoon-like idle layer. |
| **Authenticator** | Use a Certificate on an accessory for a random permanent bonus stat. Re-rolling costs 199 R$. | Endgame gear-crafting depth with a gacha feel. |
| **Diamonds as the upgrade currency** | Inventory space, selling space, advertising (+50% faster offers), tip jar (6% tip chance), price tags (+10% offers), trophy slots, storage boxes, plot expansion (30 tiles = 1,890 💎). | The premium currency also drops in play (tips, safes, quests), so free players can progress. |
| **Collections, titles, safes & trophies** | Extra goals around the core loop. | Long-tail retention. |

## Balancing notes
- **Item mutations have a wide multiplier range:** from **0.8× (Dirty)** up to **175×** for the rarest. A Dirty item being *worse* than normal is unusual and fun, because not every mutation is good.
- **Plot expansion cost rises linearly by 4 💎 per tile** (5, 9, 13, …, 65, 69, 73…), so cost is predictable.
- **Auctions are tuned to be profitable.** The beginner guide says auctions "rarely lose money", so new players aren't punished for bidding.

## UI design
- **The wiki itself uses a dark navy theme** (#1a1a2e headers), which matches the game's slick "modern business" look.
- **In-world signage is big and bold:** a black sign with green trim, a cash icon and a white uppercase label (QUICK SELL). The store is readable from far away.
- **Bidding UI:** a bar with current bid, next bid, and a moving marker over a colored target zone.

![Beginner guide banner|500](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/storagehunters/images/1/12/Homepage_Beginners_Guide.png/revision/latest)

## Models & art style
- **More realistic than most simulators:** brick storefronts, lit interiors, real-world props (TVs, guitars, furniture, cars). It looks like a "Roblox city" game.
- **Garages are themed containers** (barn garage, camo shop front, Jurassic barn), so you can guess the loot from the outside.
- **Vehicles are a big model category**, with stats for speed, handling, weight and capacity.

## Takeaways for my game
- A timing-bar minigame makes "buying" exciting.
- Have bad mutations as well as good ones (0.8×), so luck matters both ways.
- Use Net Worth as the gate, so collecting itself is progress.
- Make the premium currency also earnable through play (tips, safes, quests).
