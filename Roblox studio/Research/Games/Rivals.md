# Rivals

![Arena map|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/robloxrivals/images/3/32/Arena_Icon.png/revision/latest) ![Map top-down|260](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/robloxrivals/images/5/51/Screenshot_2025-05-10_211723.png/revision/latest)

## In one sentence
A first-person shooter where 1–5 player teams race to win **5 rounds**. You pick a loadout of primary, secondary, melee and utility weapons, and everything you can buy is cosmetic.

*This is a different genre from the others. It's included as the reference for **competitive design, weapon balancing and cosmetic-only monetization**.*

## Core loop
**Queue → vote on a map → fight rounds → earn Keys from tasks/contracts → unlock a weapon or cosmetic → queue again**

- **Duel Zone in the lobby:** step on a pad to start a match with players in the same server. There's no menu, which makes it very social.
- **Matchmaking button** for 1v1–5v5, FFA, Team Deathmatch, ranked, and **many limited-time modes** (Gun Game, Juggernaut, Spleef, "RIVALS RNG", Zombie Tower…). That's 21 modes on one weapon set.

## Popular mechanics worth stealing
| Mechanic | How it works | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **First to 5 rounds** | Short rounds and quick restarts. | The comeback potential keeps both sides engaged. |
| **Beginner 2v2** | Only for low-win players: Arena map only, and enemies are visible through walls at round start. | Protects new players from veterans, which helps retention. |
| **Ranked gating** | Requires Level 100, a 14-day-old account, verified phone/ID, 30 tasks and 10 placement matches. | Keeps smurfs and cheaters out and makes rank feel earned. |
| **Bans in ranked** | Each team bans 1 map and 2 weapons, but the default loadout can't be banned. | Counters meta weapons and adds strategy. |
| **ELO shields** | 1 free shield per day protects against one loss (not at high ranks). | Softens loss aversion for mid-ranked players. |
| **Weapon contracts** | Each weapon has goals (eliminations or playtime: 5 min → 72 h) that reward keys, wrap boxes and a unique wrap or charm for that weapon. | Encourages trying every weapon, and long goals give mastery bragging rights. |
| **Daily tasks with a streak** | Tasks reward keys, and a streak grows rewards. Missing a day resets it (refresh costs 20 R$). | Daily login habit. |
| **Win streak rewards** | Fire/blaze wraps for 10 / 100 wins in a row. A lost streak can be recovered with Keys. | A status flex, plus a key sink. |
| **Lots of game modes on one engine** | Modes reuse the same weapons and maps. | Cheap variety, and new modes keep things fresh. |

## Balancing notes
**Weapon stat sheet** (what the wiki tracks): cost, equip time, move-speed penalty, damage → falloff with range, fire cooldown, ammo, reload, spread (hip vs aim), and critical/headshot damage.

| | Assault Rifle (default) | Sniper |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | free | 75 Keys |
| Damage | 12 → 3 (falloff 50 → 200 studs) | 50 |
| Crit damage | 15 → 4 | 150 (3×, one-shot headshot) |
| Fire rate | 0.10s | 1.50s |
| Ammo | 20/100 | 4/12 |
| Reload | 1.56s | 1.80s |
| Move speed | −10% | −20% |
| Hip spread | 1° | 15° (needs to scope) |

- **Every strength has a cost:** burst damage pairs with slow fire, a heavy move penalty and bad hip-fire.
- The default weapon is "well-rounded at any range". **The free starter should be viable, not weak.**
- **Weapon types:** Standard, Prime, Contraband.

**Keys economy:**
- **Key bundles:** 10 Keys = 49 R$, 40 = 199, 90 = 449, 450 = 2,199, 1,100 = 4,999. That's the classic **bulk discount ladder** (≈4.9 → 4.5 R$ per key).
- **Beginner tasks** give 1–3 keys each, so a new player gets ~18 keys quickly. That's enough to feel progress.

## UI design
- **Clean, minimal FPS UI:** cosmetics are the flashy part, while the HUD stays readable.
- **Rarity ladder for skins:** Common / Rare / Legendary / Mythical. Mythical skins get unique sounds, effects and trails, so **higher rarity = more senses involved**.
- **The shop is split into sections** (Home, Bundles, Daily, …) with banners for the Season Pass and an "watch ad for free items" offer.
- **The daily shop has 5 rotating skins.** Common 299 R$, Rare 449, Legendary 599, or 10 Skin Tickets for any of them.

## Models & art style
- **The maps are grey-box and readable:** light grey grid-textured blocks, lots of cover, open sky. Clarity beats decoration in competitive maps.
- Many maps come in **Normal, Big and Legacy** versions, which reuses layouts for different team sizes.
- **Weapons are slightly stylized real guns.** Skins swap the whole model (e.g. AK-47, Augmented Rifle) with custom reload and inspect animations.
- **Wraps are texture overlays** on the existing models, so there are hundreds of cosmetics without new meshes.

## Takeaways for my game
- Cosmetic-only monetization works if the base game is fun and fair.
- Use wraps/skins as texture swaps, which multiplies cosmetics cheaply.
- Have a protected beginner queue.
- Make long per-item mastery contracts that reward a unique cosmetic.
