# Murder Mystery 2

![Crafting station UI|420](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/murder-mystery-2/images/c/c6/Crafting_Station.png/revision/latest)

## In one sentence
Each round one player is secretly the **Murderer** (knife), one is the **Sheriff** (gun) and everyone else is **Innocent**. Survive, collect coins, and figure out who the killer is. It has run since 2014 and is still a top game thanks to its **weapon-skin trading economy**.

*The vault's reference for **round-based social games and cosmetic trading economies**.*

## Core loop
**Lobby → round starts, roles assigned → Murderer hunts, Sheriff hunts the Murderer, Innocents hide and grab coins → round ends (max 3 min) → XP + coins → spend on boxes → trade**

- **Asymmetric roles** make every round different.
- **Innocents can become the Hero** by picking up a dead Sheriff's gun.
- **Shooting an innocent as Sheriff kills you both**, a built-in penalty for reckless play.

## Popular mechanics worth stealing
| Mechanic | How it works | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Pity system for the rare role** | Your chance to be Murderer increases every round you aren't. | Everyone gets the fun role eventually. |
| **Coins on the map** | Innocents collect coins during the round, capped at 40 (50 with Elite). | Gives the "weak" role an activity and income. |
| **Round timer = win condition** | Innocents win if anyone survives 3 minutes (5 XP/s, 900 XP for a full survive). | A clear clock creates tension. |
| **Crafting from shards** | Salvage weapons into shards. 12 Common Shards → random Uncommon, 12 Uncommon → Rare, 12 Rare → Legendary. | A sink for duplicate junk, and a way to progress without paying. |
| **Seasonal events with mystery boxes** | Holiday events with new boxes and godly packs. Every new drop reprices the whole economy. | Huge comeback spikes: a 1.35M all-time peak in August 2026 from a summer event. |
| **Trading rules** | Level 10 required (against dupes), 6-second wait after each change, 4 unique items. | Anti-scam and anti-dupe protection. |
| **Multiple modes** | Classic, Disguises, Assassin… | Variety on the same maps. |

## Balancing notes
- **Coin cap per round (40)** limits farming and keeps box prices meaningful. The cap was raised from 10–15 per round in an earlier season, and box prices were adjusted with it.
- **Innocents = players − 2**, max 10 per round.
- **The murderer gets their knife 10 seconds into the round**, giving innocents a head start.
- **Weapon rarity ladder:** Common → Uncommon → Rare → Legendary → Godly (+ Ancient/Chroma etc. at the top). Godlies are the trading currency.
- **Community-assigned values:** item worth is set by players (base value, demand, rarity, stability), not by the game. The economy runs itself.

## UI design
- **Crafting station:** a dark slate panel with a **red warning banner** at the top. Recipe rows show a rarity-colored name (pink Painted Seer, green Seer, red Legendary), a big "?" item silhouette for random rewards, material slots with counts, and a **Craft / Salvage** tab toggle. Materials and reward on the right, plus a **Confirm** button.
- **Dark red table headers** (#770000) on the wiki match the game's murder theme.
- **End-of-round results** show the Murderer's avatar with an X.

## Models & art style
- **Simple, classic Roblox maps:** houses, offices, labs, mil-base. Designed for hiding spots and sightlines rather than looks.
- **Weapons are the art.** Hundreds of knife and gun skins (~550 knives, ~300 guns on the wiki), with seasonal themes and effects.
- **Pets, effects and radios** are extra cosmetic categories (effects are untradable).

## Takeaways for my game
- Asymmetric roles plus a pity timer for the rare role works well.
- A per-round currency cap protects the economy.
- Shard crafting (12 → 1 tier up) turns duplicates into progress.
- Cosmetic economies can keep a game alive for 10+ years if trading is safe.
