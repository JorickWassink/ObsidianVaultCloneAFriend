# Pet Simulator 99

![PS99 icon|200](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/pet-simulator/images/2/22/PS99Icon.png/revision/latest) ![Area 158 top-down: breakables field|340](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/pet-simulator/images/1/14/PS99_-_Area_158_-_Circuit_Board.png/revision/latest)

## In one sentence
The 4th game in BIG Games' Pet Simulator series (launched December 2023). Your swarm of pets breaks coin piles ("breakables") in themed areas, the coins hatch eggs for stronger pets, and stronger pets let you afford the next area. This repeats across **274 areas in 4 worlds**.

*This is the textbook "classic pet sim". Most simulators copy its structure.*

## Core loop
**Pets attack breakables → coins/diamonds drop → buy the next area or hatch eggs → equip the best pets → repeat**

- **Breakables:** fields of coin piles, chests and crates. You click to target, and pets swarm the target.
- **Equip slots** limit how many pets fight, so the best X pets matter. Ranks and gamepasses add slots.
- **Each area is a little bit more expensive** and has better eggs.

## Popular mechanics worth stealing
| Mechanic | How it works | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Huge pets** | Rare, oversized pets that **scale with your strongest pet** instead of having fixed stats. Golden +0.3×, Rainbow +0.9×, Shiny +1× more damage. | Never become obsolete, so they're a lasting status symbol. |
| **Titanic / Gargantuan pets** | Even bigger tiers: Titanic = 3× your best pet, ridable, crown icon, takes 4 inventory slots. | Escalating flex tiers above "Huge". |
| **Golden / Rainbow / Shiny variants** | Fuse or roll variants of the same pet. | Same model, more collectibles. |
| **Enchants with diminishing returns** | Each extra copy of the same enchant is weaker: 100% → 60% → 38% → 27.5%. | Pushes variety in builds. |
| **Mastery (14 skills)** | Potions, Enchants, Eggs, Fishing, Digging… Each levels to 99 for permanent buffs and a cosmetic cape. All 99s = Master cape (+10% diamonds). | RuneScape-style long-term goals. |
| **Ranks with star quests** | Quests give stars; ranks unlock pet equip slots, egg-hatch slots and enchant slots. Max rank 40. | Clear guided progression with real rewards. |
| **Minigames in areas** | Obbies, fishing, digsite, minefield, each with a cooldown (1h15m–2h30m). **3 random minigames a day are "surged" for 3–10× rewards.** | Breaks up the grind, and daily surges give a reason to check in. |
| **Trading plaza + booths** | Players set up booths to sell pets. A 3-second purchase cooldown on new listings fights scams. | Player economy with personal shop customization. |
| **Clans** | Ticket 300 R$; donate diamonds to upgrade; up to 75 members; clan battles. | Social retention and a group diamond sink. |
| **Hoverboards** | Fast travel and cosmetic flex; some only for top-100 / top-10 leaderboard players or for spending 100K R$. | Mobility as a cosmetic category. |

## Balancing notes
**Area cost curve (World 1):**

| Area | Cost |
|---|---|
| 2 | 900 |
| 3 | 2.5K |
| 4 | 8K |
| 5 | 20K |
| 6 | 60K |
| 7 | 150K |
| 8 | 400K |
| 9 | 1 Gold Bar |
| 10 | 2 bars + 500K |
| 11 | 6 bars |
| 12 | 15 bars |
| 13 | 35 bars |
| 14 | 80 bars |

That's **×2.5–3 per area**. Around area 9 the game **switches currency** to Gold Bars, which resets number inflation. Later tiers use Platinum, Emerald and Void Bars.

**Egg odds pattern:** each egg typically has 3 pets at roughly **90% / 10% / tiny ("??")**. Every egg has a chase pet.

**Diamond upgrades** (61 total): Move Faster +12.5%, Magnet +3/+5, Diamonds +10%, Tap Damage +10%, Pet Speed, Drops +5%, Luck +10%, Coins +10%. Costs run 30 → 2K💎 and unlock as you reach areas.

**Enchant stacking:** additive with diminishing returns, as above.

**Rebirths:** max 9 (as of Update 79). Mastery unlocks after 4 rebirths.

**Gamepass luck:** Lucky +200% egg luck, Ultra Lucky +500%, and they stack. Magic Eggs gives a 10% Golden / 10% Rainbow chance on any hatch.

## UI design
- **Huge numbers of icons and tabs.** It's a very feature-dense UI with a quest list on the right side of the screen.
- **Rarity is shown by icon size in the inventory:** Titanic icons are about 4 normal icons big with a crown instead of stars. Size itself is a UI signal.
- **In-world shop signs** are hand-written style text on walls ("Preston's Shop! $").
- **Index Machine** in the spawn area for collection tracking.

![Preston's shop|300](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/pet-simulator/images/9/92/PS99_-_Merchant_-_Prestons_Shop.png/revision/latest) ![Index machine|260](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/pet-simulator/images/7/7e/PS99_Index_machine.png/revision/latest)

## Models & art style
- **Soft, pastel, very clean "BIG Games" style:** smooth materials, no studs, rounded shapes, bright cyan/blue tech areas.
- **Pets are cute and chibi-proportioned.** Huge and Titanic pets are scaled-up models with extra effects.
- **Areas are big flat fields** (designed for breakable spawns) surrounded by themed walls. Gameplay-first layouts.
- **Currency bars** (gold, platinum, emerald, void) are simple shiny ingots, so each new tier is easy to read.

## Takeaways for my game
- Make "huge" collectibles that scale with your best, so they never become useless.
- Switch currency every ~10 areas to reset number inflation.
- Use daily surged minigames to make players check in.
- Give enchants/buffs diminishing returns per duplicate to encourage variety.
