# Grow a Garden 2

![Baker Island event stall|420](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/growagarden27847/images/7/77/BakerIslandMain.png/revision/latest)

## In one sentence
The sequel to [[Grow a Garden]]: the same plant → harvest → sell loop, plus **night-time stealing**, **crop decay**, **guilds**, **decorative props**, and pets that spawn around the map on a timer.

*Compare this note with [[Grow a Garden]] to see what a successful sequel changes.*

## What changed from Grow a Garden
| Area | Grow a Garden | Grow a Garden 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Stealing | Paid action (37 R$) or via pets | **Free, but only at night** (2 of every ~10 minutes) |
| Crop upkeep | None | **Crops decay** (lose color) if left alone; water them or wait for rain. Mutated crops don't decay. |
| Social | Trading, gifting | **Guilds** (99 R$ to create, up to 50 members, guild quests and tokens), a **mailbox** for gifts |
| Decoration | Cosmetics | **Props shop** with grid placement, rotation and snapping |
| Pets | Eggs only | Eggs, Robux, **and pets spawning on the map with a countdown** that anyone can buy |
| Pet variants | Golden/Rainbow etc. | Size (Big 2× size, 2× passive; Mega 4× size, 3× passive) and Rainbow (0.5% per egg, ×1.25), rolled independently |
| Time | Weather events | **Day/night cycle:** 7.5 min day, 30 s sunset, 2 min night with **5 kinds of moon** (Normal 77%, Gold 13%, Rainbow 6%, Blood 2%, Mega 2%) |
| Mutations | Many, stackable | **One per crop**, 1% base chance on harvest, Gold/Rainbow seeds guarantee one |

## Popular mechanics worth stealing
| Mechanic | How it works | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Night = danger window** | Stealing is only possible at night, every cycle. | Turns the clock into a rhythm of safe farming, then tense defending. Fairer than always-on stealing. |
| **Moon roll each night** | Each night rolls a moon type, and rare moons boost mutations. | A small lottery every 10 minutes that everyone watches together. |
| **Crop decay** | Unwatered crops desaturate and decay. | Gives watering cans a purpose and rewards checking in. |
| **Map pet spawns with a timer** | Pets appear around the map with a countdown; buy them before they vanish. | A "rush to it" moment like [[Steal a Brainrot]]'s carpet. |
| **Event crafting (baking)** | The Muffin Bake event: 3 ovens, recipes from crops (e.g. 30 Blueberries + 10 Wheat + 3 Sugar Cane), 5–30 min bake times, recipes refresh hourly. | Gives crops a second use beyond selling. |
| **Guild slot upgrades** | 20 → 25 → … → 50 members, each tier costing more Robux (37 → 499). | Group monetization. |

## Balancing notes
- **Currency packs are linear:** 5K = 10 R$, 10K = 20, 25K = 50, 50K = 100, 100K = 200. That's **no bulk discount**, unlike [[Rivals]].
- **Robux skips:** Grow Plant 39 R$ (one crop), Grow All 299 R$.
- **Gear restock chances shown on the wiki:** e.g. Watering Can 90%, Common Sprinkler 50%.
- **Pet Big/Mega multipliers:** 2× / 3× passive. Rainbow is a small ×1.25 on top.
- **Base mutation chance 1% on harvest.** Weather and moons raise it.

## UI design
- The wiki has fewer UI screenshots so far (it's newer). The game keeps the original's **shop list style** (see [[Grow a Garden]]).
- **Event stalls use the same striped-awning language,** now with a brick-studded floor and big ovens (Baker Island).

## Models & art style
- **More detailed than the original:** studded brick textures, warmer lighting, chunky characterful NPCs (chef with a hat, a bear helper).
- Same bright Roblox-classic palette, but more props and set dressing.

## Takeaways for my game
- Limit PvP stealing to a recurring time window for a predictable rhythm.
- Roll a "moon" or modifier each cycle for a shared lottery moment.
- Light upkeep (decay) gives a reason to return without being punishing. Mutated items are protected.
- A sequel should keep the loop and add social layers (guilds, building).
