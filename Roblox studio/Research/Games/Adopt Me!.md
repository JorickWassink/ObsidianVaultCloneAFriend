# Adopt Me!

![5th birthday thumbnail|420](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/adoptme/images/1/10/Adopt_Me%21%27s_5th_Birthday_Thumbnail.jpg/revision/latest) ![Choose a role|300](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/adoptme/images/1/12/Adopt_Me%21_Choose_a_role.jpeg/revision/latest)

## In one sentence
A cozy family-roleplay town where you look after a baby or pets by fulfilling their needs. That earns Bucks and ages your pets, and four fully grown pets of the same kind can be **fused into a glowing Neon**, then four of those into a **Mega Neon**. Trading those pets is the real endgame.

## Core loop
**Pet/baby "ailment" appears (hungry, thirsty, sleepy, walk, ride…) → fulfil it → Bucks + pet age → buy eggs → hatch by doing tasks → age to Full Grown → fuse**

- **Care tasks are the verb.** It's a Tamagotchi in a social town.
- **Eggs hatch through tasks** (not timers), so playing equals progress. Or skip with "Hatch Now!" for 35 R$ per egg.
- **Being a baby AND having a pet doubles earnings**, which rewards using both systems.

## Popular mechanics worth stealing
| Mechanic | How it works | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Fusion ladder (4 → 1)** | 4 Full Grown → 1 Neon (glows). 4 Luminous Neon → 1 Mega Neon (rainbow-cycling glow). Ride/fly abilities carry over. | A **16× item sink** for one Mega, which keeps rare pets valuable. |
| **Age stages** | Newborn → Junior → Pre-Teen → Teen → Post-Teen → Full Grown. Neon: Reborn → Twinkle → Sparkle → Flare → Sunshine → Luminous. Rarer pets need more tasks. | Visible growth and a reason to keep caring. |
| **Ride / Fly potions** | Robux potions make a pet permanently ridable/flyable (130 / 255 R$). | Paid *upgrades on a pet you love*, which makes the pet worth more in trades. |
| **Trade license** | You must pass a test to trade Ultra-Rare/Legendary items. 18 trade slots, and a 5-second countdown resets on every change. | Anti-scam design that protects young players. |
| **Daily login with 15-hour window** | Rewards grow over 5 days (25 → 300 Bucks + stars), and you can claim early. | Flexible daily habit. |
| **Limited "gumball" eggs** | Themed eggs (Safari, Jungle, Ocean, Japan…) rotate out forever. | Scarcity drives trading value. |
| **Houses & furniture** | Build and decorate your home. | Creative long-term sink and self-expression. |

## Balancing notes
- **Daily Bucks cap: 300 per in-game day** (500 with the Pet Handler Pro certificate). Periodic **2× weekends** double both earnings and the cap.
  → An **earnings cap protects the economy** and makes Robux and trading the faster routes.
- **Paycheck:** 10 Bucks periodically, just for playing.
- **Rarities:** Common, Uncommon, Rare, Ultra-Rare, Legendary (+ Event). Rarer means more tasks to age.
- **Bucks potions:** Big Head 10, Hyperspeed 40, Anti-gravity 99, Grow 395. Cheap fun sinks.
- **Starter egg:** a free Cat or Dog for new players.

## UI design
- **Warm, friendly, kid-first:** the role picker is a **paper note pinned to a cork board** with push pins. Rounded sans font, orange (Baby) and blue (Parent) badges.
- **Task bubbles over pets and babies** show what they need, with clear icons.
- **Inventory icons for Mega Neon were changed to stop scams.** Clear visual identity of value matters in trading UIs.
- **Bucks** are shown top right.

## Models & art style
- **Polished cartoon:** pastel colors, soft lighting, cute big-eyed pets with expressive faces. Very high quality for Roblox.
- **Neon = glowing parts on the same model**; Mega Neon = color-cycling glow. A cheap but iconic variant system.
- **Thumbnails:** one huge hero pet over a blurred festive scene, with chunky white outlined title text (5th Birthday thumbnail).
- **Huge variety:** 800+ pet pages, 280+ vehicles.

## Takeaways for my game
- A 4 → 1 fusion ladder creates long-term demand for duplicates.
- Making progress by doing tasks (not timers) means playing feels productive.
- An earnings cap per day protects the economy.
- Scam-proof trading UI (countdown resets, license, distinct icons) is essential if you add trading.
