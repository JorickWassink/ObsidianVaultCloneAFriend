# Randomizer

## In one sentence
A Roblox first-person shooter (released July 2020) where you **never choose your loadout**. Every time you respawn you get 4 random items: a ranged weapon, a melee weapon, a special item and a taunt.

*The vault's reference for **wacky weapon and item ideas** and for **chaos coming from silly everyday objects** that work as real weapons. Researched for [[RNG Shooter Redesign]].*

## Core loop
**Respawn → random 4-slot loadout → adapt → die → reroll**
- The size of the item pool is the content: **112 ranged weapons, 35 melee, 33 special items**, plus taunts.
- Some items are simply stronger than others. The game accepts that, because your loadout rerolls on every death.

## Wacky ideas worth stealing
Grouped by the *kind* of joke, because the shape of the joke is what carries over to a Class.

### Everyday object, real weapon
| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Airpod Shotty | Double-barrel shotgun that "fires bass": huge damage up close, poor accuracy, long reload. |
| Banana Gun, Lime Gun (a glue gun), Bread Basket, T-Shirt Launcher, Water Gun/Pistol/Shotgun, Paintball, Slingshot, Stick, Ball | Ordinary objects in the ranged slot. Lime Gun is a reliable mid-range two-tap with upward recoil. |
| Keyboard (melee) | Hits for 30; its special is a full heal. |
| Stop Sign (melee) | Big hit (55), and it can **place a temporary wall**. |
| Umbrella (melee) | **Opens into a shield.** |
| Hotdog (melee) | 60 damage, and **you can eat it** to heal. |
| Pillow (melee) | Weak hit, but **take a short nap** for 45 health. |
| Pool Light, Wrench, Crowbar, Pitchfork, Cleaver | Plain melee from around the house. |
| Carbonated Water (special) | **Smash the bottle** and it becomes a melee weapon. |

### Food as healing
| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Chicken Leg, Sandwich, Cookie, Chocolate Bar, Hot Coco | Simple heals. |
| Honey Bottle | A heal plus a speed boost. |
| Bloxy Cola | A heal, **or drink it to self-detonate**. |
| Tablet | Pick one: temporary extra health, a speed boost or a jump boost. |

### One item, several modes (good for "more options")
| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Spike Shield | Blocks bullets, **pulls out a small gun** while held, or **charges a sprint** that deals 75 to whoever it hits and hurts you about 40 if you miss. |
| Chara's Knife | Locks your inventory, puts you in third person and **switches between a knife and a gun**. |
| Sword of Power | Badge reward: replaces your whole kit with a sword and **3 abilities**, and puts you in third person. |
| Juggernaut Box | Needs a **10-kill streak**. Turns you into an armoured boss carrying the Death Machine (a minigun with huge fire rate). |
| Cloak | Wind it up 3 times to go invisible, but a "spy" label still gives you away. |

### Risk / reward and self-damage
| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Bomb Destruct | Blow yourself up and take everyone nearby with you. |
| Spike Shield charge | Big damage on a hit, hurts you on a miss. |
| Big Iron, Golden Gun, Musket | **One-shot, one-kill** guns with a single round and a long reload. |
| Beeshot | Bee shotgun: **damage over time**. |
| Spell Book | Throws fireballs that **set enemies on fire**. Started the "not a gun" weapons. |
| Hel | Hides your name tag and **mutes your footsteps** for 10 seconds. |
| Gravity Coil, Speed Coil, Regen Coil | Classic Roblox gear as buffs. |

## Systems worth stealing
- **The silliness lives in the model and the name; the stats stay readable.** A banana gun is still clearly "a pistol".
- **Melee items with a utility special** (wall, shield, heal, nap): one object, two jobs.
- **Kill-streak unlocks** (Juggernaut at 10 kills): a power spike you have to earn inside the match.
- **Self-damage on a miss** makes big plays risky instead of free.
- **Third-person "transform" items** that swap your whole kit for a while. These are good shapes for an Ultimate.

## Takeaways for my game
- Use Class archetypes whose **props are the weapons** (janitor mop, chef pan, mascot T-shirt cannon).
- Give the Ultimates **transform** energy (Juggernaut, Sword of Power), but charged by kills, damage and time, not by a streak.
- Draw Passives and abilities from **food heals, self-damage gambles and object modes**.
- Don't copy the per-death random loadout. Our RNG happens between Matches (the Roll), not during them.
