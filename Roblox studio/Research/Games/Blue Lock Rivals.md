# Blue Lock: Rivals

![NEL King banner|420](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bluelockrivals-roblox/images/d/d3/NELKingBanner.webp/revision/latest) ![Don Lorenzo update art|420](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bluelockrivals-roblox/images/e/e3/Home_Play_-_BLR.png/revision/latest)

## In one sentence
A team soccer game based on the anime *Blue Lock*. You play a position on the field, and the character you play is decided by a **gacha spin for "Styles"** (each with unique moves and an anime **awakening**). A second spin system gives **"Flows"**, timed power-up modes.

*The vault's reference for **gacha-driven character loadouts in a PvP sports game**, and for anime-inspired VFX and presentation.*

## Core loop
**Lobby → pick a team and position on a bird's-eye field view → play (dribble, shoot, pass, tackle) → earn ¥ for goals, steals, saves, assists → spin for a better Style/Flow → play again**

- **Positions** in 5v5: Center Forward, Left/Right Wing, Center Mid, Goalkeeper. Also **8v8** and **3v3** (added Feb 2026). **Pro servers** have a buffed AI goalkeeper.
- **Your loadout = 1 Style (character/moveset) + 1 Flow (buff mode).** Both come from spins.

## Popular mechanics worth stealing
| Mechanic | How it works | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Two gacha systems** | **Style** spins (2,500 ¥) give a character with 2–3 moves and an awakening. **Flow** spins (2,000 ¥) give a buff mode. **Lucky spins** skip the low rarities. | Players chase the anime character they love, and two separate pools mean twice the chase. |
| **Pity** | Flow spins: 50 spins guarantees at least Legendary. | Softens bad luck. |
| **Flow bar** | An orange bar above the ability rack fills over time and through actions (like dribbling). Usable from 30%. Some flows (Genius) **give teammates +20–30% flow bar**. | A comeback/clutch moment every match, plus support roles. |
| **Awakening cutscenes** | Each Style has an awakening: a short anime cutscene, then a third move or replaced moves. | The "hype moment". It's very clip-able. |
| **Skill-based core** | Dribble (Q) to resist steals; slide tackles "ankle-break" (ragdoll) opponents; headers can only be chained 4 times; community "techs" like feint shots, volleys and disguised tackles. | A real skill ceiling, so the community makes guides. |
| **Rebalances and "vaulting"** | Overpowered styles are temporarily **vaulted** (removed), reworked, then returned. | Keeps the meta healthy without deleting what people own. |
| **AFK World** | A separate place where your avatar kicks a ball. Every 5 min a cash prize, every 15 min a "grand prize" (spins, emotes, rare lucky spins). It's the only free way to get lucky spins. | Retention while away, plus a visible little animation of your avatar "trying". |
| **Admin abuse twice a week** | Wednesdays and Saturdays. Devs give away 40+ lucky spins; Saturday updates come with new codes. | Appointment play, like [[Grow a Garden]] / [[Steal a Brainrot]]. |
| **Quest reward bar** | Daily quests (score goals, win matches) give 2.5K–4K ¥. Completing 8 fills a bar for 2 Lucky Flow + 2 Lucky Style spins. | A clear free-to-play path to premium spins. |

## Balancing notes
**Style spin odds** (normal / lucky):

| Rarity | Normal | Lucky |
|---|---|---|
| Rare | 62.25% | 0% |
| Epic | 30% | 0% |
| Legendary | 7% | ~91.3% |
| Mythic | 0.5% | 5% |
| World Class | 0.25% | 2.5% |
| Limited | 0.125% | — |
| Master | 0.0325% | 0.2% |

- **Flow spins** are similar: Master 0.0175% / 0.175%, World Class 0.25% / 2.5%, Mythic 0.5% / 5%.
- **A lucky spin is about 10× better** for the top tiers and removes Rare/Epic entirely.

**Cash per action:**

| Action | Cash |
|---|---|
| Steal | 10 ¥ |
| Ankle break | 10 ¥ |
| Assist | 15 ¥ |
| Goal | 20 ¥ |
| GK save | 20 ¥ |
| Catching a pass | 40 ¥ |
| Daily quest milestone | 1,000 ¥ |
| Day 4 daily reward | 10,000 ¥ |

- **VIP doubles cash.**
- **One Style spin = 2,500 ¥, so roughly a full quest or several matches per spin.**
- AFK World cash is listed differently on two wiki pages (250 vs 500 ¥ per 5 min). Treat it as unconfirmed.

**Cosmetic packs** (emote / player card / goal effect):
- 1,200 / 1,800 / 2,000 ¥, or 99 / 149 / 199 R$.
- **Odds differ by currency:** with ¥, Rare 84% / Epic 15% / Legendary 1%. With Robux, 0% Rare / 65% Epic / 35% Legendary.
  → **Paying Robux removes the bottom tier** instead of selling items directly.

**Other prices:** starter pack 99 R$ (30,000 ¥ + a Legendary goal effect + 3 Lucky Style spins). Ranked needs Level 20 to keep alts and bots out.

**Rarity ladder:** Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythic → World Class → Master (+ Limited). The top tier is named after the sport ("World Class"), not generic fantasy words.

## UI design
![Halloween lobby|380](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bluelockrivals-roblox/images/a/af/Hallowlobby.png/revision/latest) ![Hallow spins area|380](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/bluelockrivals-roblox/images/0/06/Hallowspins.png/revision/latest)

- **The lobby is a physical menu.** Big glowing 3D signs over terminals (PLAY, the event, "HALLOW SPINS", "8v8", "NEXT UPDATE SIGN UP") with a countdown to the event finale on a screen. You walk to what you want.
- **Neon "sports arena" look:** a dark hall with orange and purple neon strips and floor arrows guiding you toward the play terminal.
- **The gacha is a place, not just a menu:** a spin area with glowing platforms and a billboard advertising the newest style.
- **Match HUD:** score bar at the top, ability rack and flow bar above the hotbar at the bottom, a name/stamina plate under the player.
- **The wiki itself** uses black panels with cyan neon glow and the Rubik font, the same "esports anime" mood.

## Models & art style
- **Avatars styled as anime characters:** Roblox block bodies with custom hair, faces and team kits (Blue Lock blue, NEL black-and-white). Recognizable from the hairstyle alone.
- **Banners and update art are full anime illustration:** heavy VFX, flames, lightning, a giant spirit animal behind the player (a fire lion for NEL King), bold manga-style text. **Marketing art is far more dramatic than gameplay.**
- **Gameplay visuals are clean:** a plain green field with white lines, so the ball and players are readable, with flashy VFX only on abilities and awakenings.
- **Seasonal reskins** (Halloween, Snow, Easter) of both lobby and styles.

## Takeaways for my game
- Gacha for *characters you fight as* works in PvP if skill still matters most.
- Use two separate spin pools (character + buff) for a deeper chase.
- Make Robux packs better by removing the bottom tier rather than selling items directly.
- Give everyone a "flow bar" for a clutch, comeback moment every match.
- Temporarily vault overpowered items instead of nerfing them into the ground.
- Make the lobby physical, with glowing signs and walk-up terminals.
