# Fisch

![Fisch thumbnail|500](https://static.wikitide.net/fischwiki/b/b0/Fischthumbnail.jpg)

## In one sentence
An open-world fishing RPG. You cast, play a reeling minigame, sell fish for coins, buy better rods and boats, and sail to new islands to complete the **Bestiary** of hundreds of fish (the game claims "400,000+ variations").

## Core loop
**Cast (hold to charge distance) → Shake to lure faster → "!" bite → reel minigame → sell to merchant → upgrade rod → new location**

- Starts at **Moosewood** with a tutorial NPC (Pierre).
- **A boat (Rowboat, 400 C$) is the first big goal.** It opens the ocean between islands.
- **75 locations** (29 major + 49 sub-locations), plus 16 limited-time locations that ran during past events.

## Popular mechanics worth stealing
| Mechanic | How it works | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Two-step fishing minigame** | 1) Optional **Shake** button presses cut the lure time. 2) **Reel bar:** hold to move your bar right, release to fall left, and keep the fish inside your bar to fill progress. | Simple controls with real skill involved, and it works on mobile, PC and console. |
| **Perfect catch** | Keep the fish in the bar the whole time for 1.5× XP and a C$ bonus. | Gives skilled players something to aim for. |
| **Bite sound by rarity** | The "!" prompt plays a different sound depending on rarity. | Builds anticipation before you even see the fish, and you learn to love that sound. |
| **Rod stats** | Lure Speed (max 99), Luck, Control, Resilience… | Rods have trade-offs, so there's no single "best" rod at each stage. |
| **Enchanting via Relics** | Rare relic catches (1/350) enchant a rod with a random bonus. | A gacha layer on gear with a long tail. |
| **Appraisal** | Pay an NPC to re-roll a fish's mutation and size. | A sink for coins and gambling excitement. |
| **Mutations + stackable attributes** | One mutation per fish, plus stacking attributes like Shiny, Sparkling (1.85×), Big or Giant. | Huge variety from the same fish models. |
| **Time, weather, seasons** | A full day is 25 min. Fish outside their preferred time spawn 75% less often. Seasons last ~9.6 hours (+25% for in-season fish). Totems can change weather or time. | The world feels alive, and it gives reasons to come back at different times. |
| **Level-gated endgame rods** | Late rods require Level 200 / 350 / 750 plus money. | Playtime progression, not just money. |

## Balancing notes
**Rarity ladder:** Trash → Common → Uncommon → Unusual → Rare → Legendary → Mythical → Exotic → Secret, plus special classes (Relic, Fragment, Gemstone, Extinct, Limited). Trash at the bottom is a nice touch: **failure still gives you something** (and comedy).

**Example rod progression** (from the community guide):
- Rowboat 400 C$
- Midas Rod 55K
- Fallen Rod 175K
- Rod of the Depths 750K
- Then level-gated rods from 1.25M to 5M C$ at Levels 200 → 750

**Lure formula:** `T = Tbase × (1 − LureSpeed/100)`, with Lure Speed capped at 99.

**Mutation priority rule:** a fish holds only one mutation, and the higher multiplier wins. If total chances go over 100%, the lowest ones are dropped. That's a clean way to handle stacking boosts.

**Rarity drives difficulty as well as value.** Rarer fish fight harder in the minigame.

## UI design
![Progression guide summary (community-made)|600](https://static.wikitide.net/fischwiki/3/3a/Progression_Guide.png)

- **Dark translucent panels** with colored rod pills grouped by level range. Tier colors map to a legend. *(This is a community-made chart, but it shows how readable tier-colored pills are.)*
- **Hold-to-cast bar on the right side** of the player, and a big centered "Shake" button that's mobile-friendly.
- **Day/night indicator** in the bottom right.

## Models & art style
- **Atmospheric and realistic-ish lighting:** foggy docks, soft depth of field, water reflections. That's much moodier than typical simulators. The thumbnail pairs a classic yellow noob with a big low-poly fish and a chunky yellow logo font.
- **Fish are low-poly stylized versions of real species,** plus fantasy ones. Mutations change color/material or add particles (some are animated GIFs on the wiki).
- **Rods are the main collectible model:** each has a distinct silhouette and effect (gold, ice, magma, cosmic…).
- **Islands each have a strong biome identity:** pine docks, tropical, volcanic, deep sea, jungle, forest.

## Takeaways for my game
- A two-phase minigame (prepare, then skill check) feels great and works across devices.
- Use rarity-specific audio cues.
- Day, night and seasons change what's available, which gives cheap variety.
- Trash as the lowest rarity makes failure funny.
- Atmosphere (fog, lighting) makes a simple loop feel premium.
