# Blox Fruits

![Stats menu|380](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/roblox-blox-piece/images/c/c2/NewStats.jpeg/revision/latest) ![Level UI|300](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/roblox-blox-piece/images/9/92/MaxUltimateLevel.jpeg/revision/latest)

## In one sentence
A big open-world pirate RPG inspired by One Piece. You level up by fighting NPCs on islands, put stat points into one of five combat paths, and eat **Blox Fruits** that give superpowers, across three huge "seas".

*The vault's main reference for **RPG progression, combat builds and long-form content.***

## Core loop
**Quest giver → kill X enemies → EXP + Beli → level up (+3 stat points) → buy/upgrade fruit, sword, gun or fighting style → move to a higher-level island**

- **Choose a team** at start: Pirates or Marines, each with its own starter island.
- **Five stat paths:** Melee, Defense, Sword, Gun, Blox Fruit. **You can only max 3 of 5**, so builds are real choices.
- **Three seas** gated by level: First (1–700), Second (700–1500), Third (1500+).

## Popular mechanics worth stealing
| Mechanic | How it works | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Fruits = powers** | Each fruit gives 4–5 unique moves, with a weakness (taking damage in water). Bought from a dealer with **rotating stock**, from a gacha, from trading, or as **world spawns every hour** (45 min on weekends). | Gacha excitement plus a trading economy around the most-wanted fruits. |
| **Weapon classes with move counts** | Fighting styles have 3 moves, fruits 4–5, swords and guns 2. Swords can be upgraded at a blacksmith and enchanted. | Clear differences between weapon types. |
| **Races** | Random at first join: Human 62.5%, Rabbit/Shark/Angel 12.5% each. Secret races come from puzzles or quests. Re-roll for 90 R$. | Identity, re-roll monetization, and secret content. |
| **Island secrets raise the level cap** | +5 levels per secret found in the First Sea. | Rewards exploration with real power. |
| **Raids** | 5 islands of escalating enemies plus a boss. They give Fragments (300–1,000) to "awaken" fruit moves. Level 700 to join, 1,100 to host. | Co-op endgame content and a second currency. |
| **Bounty / honor PvP** | Earn bounty for PvP kills. At high bounty you lose more on death (2× above 4M). Cap at 30M. | Risk grows with fame, so top players are targets. |
| **Long secret quests** | e.g. Advanced Combat: visit islands in a secret order told by an NPC's stories. | Community "how do I unlock X" content keeps the game talked about. |

## Balancing notes
- **EXP to next level** = `2 × level^2.3 + 84` (wiki example for level 100). That's a polynomial curve, steeper than linear but not exponential.
- **3 stat points per level**, and each stat is capped at the max level. At max, a stat is ~`maxLevel × 0.028×` stronger than at 0 points.
- **Stat effects:** Melee +0.5 damage and +5 energy per point; Defense +5 HP; Sword and Gun +0.5 damage.
- **Rarity of swords:** Common 3, Uncommon 5, Rare 9… (42 total). Few at the bottom.
- **Bounty math:** max gain 21K, max loss 42K per kill. You can only gain bounty from players within 25% of your level, which stops farming low-level players.

## UI design
- **The stats panel** is the clearest example of a "spend points" UI: a row per stat with an icon in the stat's color (Melee red fist, Defense blue shield, Sword green, Gun orange, Fruit purple), "Lv. 2804 (MAX)", and a yellow **+1** button. A "Custom Amount" input, a Stat Points counter and a **Refund** button.
- **Header bar** in bright orange-yellow with a bold white title, a blue ? help button and a red X.
- **Each stat has its own color**, used across icons and text.

## Models & art style
- **Stylized anime adventure:** colorful islands, cel-ish lighting, big readable NPCs and bosses.
- **The First Sea was revamped (Update 30)** with detailed buildings and interiors. Big games keep upgrading old areas.
- **Fruits are iconic models** (each fruit has a unique pattern and color), and their moves are flashy VFX. **VFX are the main art investment.**

## Takeaways for my game
- "Max 3 of 5 stats" forces real build choices.
- Hourly world spawns of rare items give everyone a chance.
- Hide secrets that raise the level cap, so exploring is rewarded with power.
- Risk/reward PvP: the more famous you are, the more you lose.
